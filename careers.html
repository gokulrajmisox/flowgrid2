ABOUT FLOWGRID

Flowgrid is a creative technology and connected business systems studio based in India, designing custom web experiences, operational workspaces, client communication tools, and AI voice automation.

Our internship program is built around hands-on execution, close collaboration, and practical skill growth across 1-month or 2-month tracks in Remote or Hybrid mode.

Program terms: Flowgrid internships are unpaid project-based learning programs. Submitting an application does not guarantee selection—if selected, the internship is free of cost; if not selected, a fee of Rs. 500 applies. Every successful participant receives an official completion certificate.

Questions about an open role or ready to explore our wider studio work? Reach out directly or visit the rest of Flowgrid.
